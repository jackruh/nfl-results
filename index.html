How picks are made and graded

The rule. About 24 hours before kickoff, each player prop at US books is compared with Pinnacle's price on the same line, with Pinnacle's margin removed. A pick is posted when a US book's price beats that fair price by 3% or more in expected value. No football model is involved.

Closing-line value. Pinnacle's no-vig probability at kickoff for the side taken, minus the break-even probability of the posted price, in percentage points. Positive means the market moved toward the pick. It is the main measure, because results over a few hundred bets are mostly luck.

Interval. Normal approximation, mean ± 1.96 standard errors. Under 200 graded picks, no figure here proves anything.

Status. Paper only. Nothing is staked until the rule passes a pre-declared gate: positive closing-line value with an interval above zero over 200 or more picks.
